SHOT FIELDS, EXTENDED
The meanings of the extended fields, sent once in the conversation, with a shot read in detail. The preamble and group notes of the SHOT FIELDS section of the system prompt apply to them too.

[Checks]
- Held checks [extended]: The expectations of the confirmed signature that held on this shot, the context expectations whatever they came to (they inform and never raise the badge), and the free-text ones, which only the per-shot reading checks and are never decided by a number. Said only when there is something to say.

[Outcome]
- Yield against target [extended]: The yield as a share of the target yield of the version the shot is filed under, in %. Worked out when the shot is read: refile the shot and it moves. Needs a scale and a version with a target.
- Phases not reached [extended]: The phases of the shot's profile that never began, by name: the shot ended before them. Absent when every phase began, or when the shot has no profile.
- Phase log [extended]: Said once when the log cannot tell how the phases went: a log from before firmware log version 5 has no phase table, so there are no per-phase numbers; version 5 has one but not why each phase ended.

[Timing]
- Pre-infusion time [extended]: Seconds until pressure first reached half its peak, read off the pressure trace rather than the phase names: when wetting ended and extraction began. Needs a pressure sensor.
- Main extraction time [extended]: Seconds from the end of pre-infusion (as above) to the end of the shot. Needs a pressure sensor.

[Temperature]
- Average temperature [extended]: The mean measured brew water temperature over the whole shot, in °C.
- Target temperature [extended]: The mean temperature the profile asked for over the shot, in °C. The machine heats to the profile's temperature; the Set records none of its own.
- Minimum temperature [extended]: The lowest measured temperature during the shot, in °C.
- Maximum temperature [extended]: The highest measured temperature during the shot, in °C.

[Pressure]
- Average pressure [extended]: The mean pressure over the whole shot, in bar.
- Minimum pressure [extended]: The lowest pressure over the whole shot, in bar.
- Time of peak pressure [extended]: Seconds into the shot when the peak pressure was reached.

[Flow and volume]
- Average flow [extended]: The mean puck flow over the whole shot, pre-infusion included, in ml/s. Puck flow is the machine's estimate of the water passing through the coffee.
- Peak flow [extended]: The highest puck flow during the shot, in ml/s.
- Total volume [extended]: Puck flow integrated over the shot, in ml: the water that passed through the puck. More than the yield, because the puck keeps some.
- Water pumped [extended]: The water the pump moved, in ml, from the machine's own count; only for machines whose pump counts it.

[Weight]
From the scale's readings: none of these exists without a scale.
- Water pumped minus beverage weight [extended]: Water pumped less the beverage weight, in g; needs both.
- Average weight rate [extended]: How fast weight arrived in the cup over the brew phases, in g/s, from the scale's readings (steps where the weight fell are left out).

[Puck resistance]
- Resistance slope [extended]: The slope of the resistance over the brew phases, per second: negative is a resistance falling through the shot, positive one rising. A number, not a grade.
- Machine puck resistance (s·√bar/mL) [extended]: The firmware analyzer's machine puck resistance pr (s·√bar/mL): average, then first, last, lowest, highest. The square root of the level's quantity, so not comparable with it.
- Liquid resistance (bar·s/mL) [extended]: The firmware analyzer's liquid resistance, pr · √pressure (bar·s/mL): average, then first, last, lowest, highest.

[Profile compliance]
- Largest pressure undershoot [extended]: The most measured pressure fell below the target pressure, in bar. A large one throughout is a puck too loose to build pressure.

[Phases]
- type [extended]: What the engine took the phase to be. preinfusion when its name contains preinfusion, pre-infusion, pi, soak, bloom, fill, preinfuse; decline when it contains decline, taper, ramp-down, ramp down, cool down, cooldown; otherwise read from the pressure trace (a first phase under 5 bar and rising is preinfusion, a last phase falling faster than 0.3 bar per sample is decline), else brew. A description only: every phase gets the same metrics. Every phase but a preinfusion one is one of the shot's brew phases.
- start [extended]: Seconds into the shot when the phase began.
- pressure [extended]: The mean pressure during the phase, in bar.
- peak pressure [extended]: The highest pressure during the phase, in bar.
- pressure at end [extended]: The pressure at the phase's last sample, in bar.
- temperature [extended]: The mean measured temperature during the phase, in °C.
- lowest temperature [extended]: The lowest measured temperature during the phase, in °C.
- target temperature [extended]: The mean temperature the profile asked for during the phase, in °C.
- volume [extended]: Puck flow integrated over the phase, in ml.
- flow [extended]: The mean puck flow during the phase, in ml/s.
- peak flow [extended]: The highest puck flow during the phase, in ml/s.
- scale flow [extended]: The mean of the scale's flow readings during the phase, in g/s: how fast the cup filled. Needs a scale.
- peak scale flow [extended]: The highest scale flow reading during the phase, in g/s. Needs a scale.
- cup gained [extended]: How much the cup gained during the phase, in g: its weight at the phase's end less its weight at the previous phase's end (0 g before the first). Needs a scale.
- water pumped [extended]: How much the pump's own water counter rose during the phase, in ml. Samples after the counter is reset (it is zeroed when a weight stop ends the shot) are not read. Absent where the counter was not recorded.
- first drip [extended]: Seconds into the shot of the first sample with any puck flow, on the phase it fell in.
- pressure adherence [extended]: Pressure adherence (as above) within a pressure-steered phase, in bar. Needs a pressure sensor and the shot's profile.
- flow error [extended]: Flow-steered phases only: the root-mean-square difference between pump flow and the flow target within the phase, in ml/s.
- ramp [extended]: Pre-infusion phases only: how fast pressure rose over the phase (its least-squares slope), in bar/s.
- saturation [extended]: Pre-infusion phases only: seconds into the phase until flow settled (three samples flowing over 0.1 ml/s and varying by under 0.15): when the puck was wet through. The whole phase when it never settled.
- taper [extended]: Decline phases only: how fast pressure fell (its slope, bar/s), then how evenly (the standard deviation of its sample-to-sample rate, bar/s; lower is smoother).
- resistance [extended]: The puck resistance within the phase, as the shot's resistance level is, with where it came from (the machine's own measurement, or computed). Only a phase with flow to divide by has one.
- resistance slope [extended]: The slope of the puck resistance within the phase, per second, as the shot's resistance slope is.
- machine puck resistance (s·√bar/mL) [extended]: The machine puck resistance over this phase, as the shot's: average, then first, last, lowest, highest. Every phase.
- liquid resistance (bar·s/mL) [extended]: The liquid resistance over this phase, as the shot's.

[Curve]
One table: a line saying how many of the shot's samples it holds, a header naming each column and its unit, then one comma-separated row per sample in time order. A long shot is cut to a few dozen rows chosen to keep the curve's shape (peaks, dips and turns of pressure and puck flow), and the line says which moments are always kept: the first and last sample, each phase's first and last, peak pressure, first drip and both ends of the largest pressure drop. A short shot is whole. Every column is cut at the same rows. An empty cell was not recorded; a channel the machine did not record is left out.
- pressure (bar) [extended]: Measured pressure at the puck, in bar. Absent without a pressure sensor.
- target pressure (bar) [extended]: The pressure the profile commanded (or its limit, on a flow-led phase), in bar.
- puck flow (ml/s) [extended]: The machine's estimate of water flowing through the puck, in ml/s.
- target flow (ml/s) [extended]: The flow the profile commanded (or its limit, on a pressure-led phase), in ml/s.
- weight (g) [extended]: The weight in the cup as the scale read it, in grams. Absent without a scale.
- temperature (°C) [extended]: The measured brew water temperature, in °C.
- phase marker [extended]: The number of the profile phase running at that sample, as in the phase lines.

[Review]
The newest finished review of the shot, or nothing when it was never reviewed. A review is a model's reading of this one shot's data, made without the person's judgement, the Set or any other shot: weigh it below the measured numbers and below the person's judgement.
- Predicted balance [extended]: What a review expects the cup's balance to be: Sour, Balanced or Bitter. Written by a model from this shot's data, without the person's judgement: a reading, not a measurement.
- Predicted body [extended]: What a review expects the cup's body to be: thin, medium or heavy. Written by a model from this shot's data, without the person's judgement: a reading, not a measurement.
- Prediction confidence [extended]: How sure the review said it was of its taste prediction: low, medium or high. Written by a model from this shot's data, without the person's judgement: a reading, not a measurement.
- Review description [extended]: A paragraph on what the shot's telemetry shows and why, with its figures. Written by a model from this shot's data, without the person's judgement: a reading, not a measurement.
- Review summary [extended]: The review in one sentence. Written by a model from this shot's data, without the person's judgement: a reading, not a measurement.
- Review written [extended]: When the review finished, in UTC, to the minute. Written by a model from this shot's data, without the person's judgement: a reading, not a measurement.
- Review model [extended]: The model that wrote the review, as the provider named it. Written by a model from this shot's data, without the person's judgement: a reading, not a measurement.
